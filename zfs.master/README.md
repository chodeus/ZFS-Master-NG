#### ZFS Master NG for Unraid

The ZFS Master NG plugin provides information and control over the ZFS Pools in your Unraid server. Available ZFS Pools are listed under the "Main/ZFSMaster" tab.

Requires Unraid 6.9.2 or newer with a ZFS pool. Before 6.12, ZFS comes from the ZFS plugin. Support: https://github.com/chodeus/ZFS-Master-NG/issues
