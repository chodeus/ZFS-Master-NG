#### ZFS Master NG for Unraid

See and manage your ZFS pools, datasets and snapshots from the **Main** tab: pool health, dataset properties, snapshots, encryption and directory conversion.

Requires Unraid 6.9.2 or newer with a ZFS pool. Before 6.12, ZFS comes from the ZFS plugin.

Installs over ZFS Master and keeps your settings. Help and bug reports: https://github.com/chodeus/ZFS-Master-NG/issues
